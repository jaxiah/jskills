---
name: grill-me
description: Interview the user rigorously about a plan or design until reaching shared understanding of the material decisions. Use when the user wants to stress-test an idea, think through a design, or explicitly asks to be grilled.
---

Interview me rigorously about the material decisions in this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down consequential branches of the decision tree one at a time, resolving dependencies between decisions. Do not expand the interview with low-impact questions. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
